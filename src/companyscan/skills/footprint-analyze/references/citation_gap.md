# Citation gap

Source: `technical/citation_gap.json`. It takes the pages `technical/ai_search.json` cited in its unbranded buyer answers, fetches each third-party one once, and records whether it mentions the business. A page counts as mentioning it when its text or links match the identity `fingerprint`: the domain, an official profile link, a business name or a person's name. The match is a literal string check done in code, so a match is OBSERVED. What it means for AI visibility is INFERRED.

- **The headline:** out of the third-party pages the engine cited for this market, how many mention the business (`summary.mentioned` of `summary.third_party`). Also say how many of the cited pages are the business's own (`owned`: its site or official profiles). Pages the engine cites are the evidence it builds answers from, so a business missing from them is missing from the answers' sources. Don't claim that being mentioned on a page causes a recommendation. No ranking factor is known.
- **The gap list:** the `not_mentioned` pages with the most `answers`, with their `domain`, `title` and the `prompts` that led to them. These are the corroboration targets: directories, review sites, "best X" roundups, associations and news. Name the top ones in recommendations, and say what kind of page each is from its title and domain. A cited page on a competitor's own site (it appears in `ai_search` competitors, or it is plainly a rival firm's insights page) is not a target. It shows whose content the engine treats as the authority, which points to publishing comparable content rather than getting listed. Report those separately from third-party targets. `domains[].mentioned` rolls pages up by domain.
- **Match strength:** `match: "strong"` means a link to the site or an official profile, or the domain in the text. `match: "name_only"` means only a name matched, and it could be a namesake. Quote the `snippet` and say it is unconfirmed. A mention can also be negative, so read the snippet before calling it good.
- **What's missing:** `unreachable`, `blocked_by_robots`, `unreadable` and `not_checked` pages say nothing either way. Report the counts as a coverage limit, and never count them as gaps. If most pages were unreachable, say the result is thin.
- **A thin fingerprint:** if `fingerprint.names` has only the domain's label and `people` and `profiles` are empty, many real mentions can be missed. Recommend filling in the site profile (people, other names, official profiles) and re-crawling.

If `status` is `UNKNOWN` (`ai_search_not_collected`), say that citation gap needs AI search in the same run, and stop. Use the verdicts and citation shape from [analysis-rubric.md](analysis-rubric.md). Cite `technical/citation_gap.json` with the page `url` as the locator and the `snippet` as the quote. Give every finding a `business_impact` per [business-impact.md](business-impact.md); the loss is usually `visibility`. Finding IDs use an `N` prefix. Add a `citation_gap` object `{verdict, summary, cited_third_party, mentioned, top_gaps, findings}` to `analysis.json` (`top_gaps`: up to 10 not-mentioned domains, as strings) and a **Citation gap** section to `report.md`.
